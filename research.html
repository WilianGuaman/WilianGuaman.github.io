Research

Power systems, energy planning and applied electrical engineering

My research combines mathematical optimization, power-system analysis, data-driven methods and experimental work. The projects below show the institutions and topics that have shaped this work.

Main research line

Long-term planning of hydro-dependent power systems

My PhD thesis developed PARAMO, a framework that links investment decisions with monthly operation, hydrological uncertainty, reservoir constraints and transmission reinforcement.

Thesis: Long-Term Planning of Hydro-Dependent Power Systems: A Modeling Framework for Sustainable Energy Transition.

The work addresses generation and transmission expansion, reconductoring, reservoir operation, reserve, demand evolution, policy pathways and uncertainty within a common optimization framework. Ecuador is the principal case study.

Energy Conversion and Management: X · PARAMO Ecuador Results Explorer

Related research

The doctoral work also produced studies on demand forecasting, transmission expansion, reconductoring, reinforcement learning for planning and reactive compensation. Collaboration with the Division of Energy Economics at the Mineral and Energy Economy Research Institute of the Polish Academy of Sciences contributed to several of these outputs.

Teaching platform

POSEP

POSEP is the platform I use in Power System Planning and Operation courses. It brings together theoretical material, system datasets, optimization exercises, AMPL models and AI-assisted analysis.

Students move from the technical formulation of a problem to its computational solution and then interpret the result from an engineering perspective.

Teaching approach

Technical foundations and applied work

I use case studies, simulation and optimization when they help students understand how a model behaves outside a simplified classroom exercise. Thesis supervision is another part of this work, particularly in planning, operation, renewables, high voltage and electrical-system analysis.

Course design

From models to decisions

Assignments increasingly combine data preparation, model formulation, software implementation and technical discussion. The aim is to make the computational result understandable as an engineering decision rather than as an isolated numerical answer.

Professional development

Teaching and technical competencies

Selected training is grouped around the skills that support my teaching and applied work.

Instructional design and assessment

Online course design, active methodologies, formative and summative assessment, rubrics, feedback and development of teaching materials. Selected formal training includes the International Course in Teaching Competencies and Design of Didactic Materials for Online Education at Universidad Internacional de La Rioja (125 hours, 5 ECTS).

Digital teaching and AI

Use of virtual learning environments, digital resources, Microsoft tools and AI-assisted workflows for teaching, academic organization and development of learning activities. Training includes the international course Eficiencia Docente 4.0.

Power-system analysis and optimization

Power-system planning, mathematical optimization, optimal power flow, economic dispatch, Python, simulation software and computational analysis applied to electrical networks.

Renewable energy and applied engineering

Photovoltaic systems, renewable-energy applications, high-voltage engineering and practical analysis of electrical installations and energy projects.

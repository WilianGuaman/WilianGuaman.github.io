Teaching platform

POSEP

POSEP is a teaching platform for Power System Planning and Operation. I use it to connect theoretical content with datasets, optimization exercises, AMPL models and AI-supported workflows.

The emphasis is on moving from formulation to solution and then discussing whether the result is technically meaningful for the electrical system being studied.

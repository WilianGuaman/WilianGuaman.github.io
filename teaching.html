Teaching platform

POSEP

POSEP is the platform I use in Power System Planning and Operation courses. It brings together theory, system data, optimization exercises, AMPL models and AI-assisted analysis.

The platform is used to move from problem formulation to computational solution and then to the technical interpretation of the result.

Technical foundations and applied work

I use case studies, simulation and optimization when they help students understand how a model behaves outside a simplified classroom exercise. Thesis supervision is another part of this work, particularly in planning, operation, renewables, high voltage and electrical-system analysis.

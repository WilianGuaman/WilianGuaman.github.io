Wilian Guamán Cuenca

I am an engineer, researcher and energy consultant with experience in power systems, energy projects and higher education. My professional work has included the coordination, execution and supervision of electrical infrastructure, surface facilities, maintenance and commissioning activities for generation projects. In academia, my work covers power-system planning and operation, generation and transmission expansion, renewable integration, optimization, forecasting, high-voltage engineering and applied computational methods.

Professor in Electrical Engineering at ESPOCH and Coordinator of the Master’s in Renewable Energy and Energy Sustainability. PhD in Electrical Engineering, Power and Energy, from ESPOL.

Research interests

My research ranges from long-term power-system planning to applied electrical engineering, combining optimization, simulation, data analysis and experimental work.

Power-system planning and optimization

Generation and transmission expansion, reservoirs, reconductoring, uncertainty and policy pathways.

Power-system operation and resilience

Reactive compensation, network security, flexibility and operating conditions.

Data-driven power systems

Demand forecasting, machine learning and reproducible computational analysis.

Applied electrical engineering

High-voltage testing, power electronics, renewable-energy systems and microgrids.

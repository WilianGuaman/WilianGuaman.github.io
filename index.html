Doctoral research

Long-term planning of hydro-dependent power systems

The dissertation develops a planning framework that tests investment decisions against monthly operating conditions, reservoir constraints and hydrological uncertainty.

Research focus

The work links generation and transmission expansion, reconductoring, reservoir operation, reserve, network constraints and policy pathways in a common optimization workflow. Ecuador is used as the principal case study.

Research collaboration

The doctoral work is based at ESPOL-FIEC, with collaboration from the Division of Energy Economics at the Mineral and Energy Economy Research Institute of the Polish Academy of Sciences. PLGrid / ACK Cyfronet AGH resources have supported multi-scenario computation and post-processing.

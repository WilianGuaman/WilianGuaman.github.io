IEEE SIGHT Ecuador

Angamarca: technology, production and STEM education

I served as professional coordinator of the IEEE SIGHT component of “Transforming vulnerable communities in the Ecuadorian Andes”. The work in Angamarca combined a grain mill, an automated grain-drying initiative and STEM activities for children and adolescents.

Water and automation

Automatic chlorination system in Licto

ESPOCH-ONE implemented an automatic chlorination system for the rural drinking-water supply in Licto, Chimborazo. The project covered electrical infrastructure, grounding, dosing-system integration, commissioning, operator training and handover to the local water board. It was completed in July 2026.

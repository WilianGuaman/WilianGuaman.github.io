Community work has given me a way to connect engineering education with real technical needs, particularly through IEEE humanitarian-technology projects and ESPOCH outreach.

IEEE Tech4Good · 2025–2026

ESPOCH-ONE

Project Lead. ESPOCH-ONE addresses the automatic chlorination of the rural drinking-water system in Licto, Chimborazo. IEEE Tech4Good awarded the project a USD 9,998 grant.

The work has included technical inspections, water-quality analysis, soil-resistivity measurements, grounding design, electrical service extensions, metering, electrical installations, civil and hydraulic coordination, dosing-system integration and preparation for commissioning.

IEEE SIGHT Ecuador · 2024

Transforming vulnerable communities in the Ecuadorian Andes

I served as professional coordinator of the IEEE SIGHT component developed in Angamarca. The project combined a community grain mill, an automated grain-drying initiative and STEM activities for children and adolescents.

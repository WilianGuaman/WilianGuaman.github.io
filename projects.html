Research framework

PARAMO

Optimization framework for long-term planning of hydro-dependent power systems. It represents generation and transmission expansion, reconductoring, reservoirs, hydrological uncertainty, reserve, costs, emissions and adequacy within a common planning model.

Foundational paperResearch context
Interactive research output

PARAMO Ecuador Results Explorer

Public explorer for the Ecuador 24-bus national planning case and a 6-bus demonstrator. It provides direct access to scenario results for expansion, operation, hydrology, costs, emissions, reliability and uncertainty.

Teaching platform

POSEP

Platform used in Power System Planning and Operation courses. It combines theoretical material, datasets, optimization problems, AMPL models and AI-assisted workflows for technical analysis.
